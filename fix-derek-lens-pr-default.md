### Derek's lens still said a PR waits for Dave · Fix · 2026-07-29

Derek's repo lens had missed the July 27, 2026 decision that a PR opens, merges, and folds in one
motion by default. Three places still described the old rule — *"Chris reports it and waits for Dave's
word"*, *"Only at Dave's explicit direction — Derek never opens a PR on his own initiative"*, and a
merge paragraph resting on that same command — while [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), Chris's lens, Derek's
portable persona body, and the `open-pr` skill had all been updated. Found while reading the lens to
route an unrelated job.

Nothing had gone wrong, because the constitution outranks a lens. But this is exactly the drift a
session trips over: a specialist reading his own lens would have stopped and asked Dave for permission
the constitution says is not needed, and the contradiction is invisible unless you happen to read both
files in one sitting. The lens now states the default, names the two exceptions (a visible result;
irreversible or outward-facing work) with a link to the safety rules, and keeps the note that an
explicit "open the PR" still authorises the whole movement.

**The general lesson, worth more than this one fix:** when a portable playbook and its repo lens both
describe the same rule, changing the portable side does not change the lens — and no gate compares
them, because a lens is *supposed* to differ from the playbook. A cross-cutting decision has to be
walked through every lens that restates it, at the moment it is taken.
